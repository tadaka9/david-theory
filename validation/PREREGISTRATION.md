# Protocol to complete BEFORE real-world verification

- Title, investigators, date, version, and commit:
- Field and falsifiable question:
- Status of the formula (identity / deterministic prediction / conditional mean):
- Independent definitions of E, h, and H; common unit and calibration:
- System boundaries, period, population, and inclusion/exclusion criteria:
- Source, rights, instruments, reliability, and measurement error:
- Sampling design: iid, temporal, clustered, or repeated measures:
- Justified sample size, precision/power, and stopping rule:
- Split, dependence handling, and separation of confirmation data:
- Models, metrics, domain-scale margins, and rejection criteria:
- Bootstrap/inference, repetitions, and multiple-comparison correction:
- Missing data, outliers, and prespecified sensitivity analyses:
- What would constitute falsification:
- Publication plan for negative results and protocol deviations:
- Independent replication team and data repository:

For mathematics, replace the statistical section with the space or axioms, statement, quantifiers, assumptions, and proof or counterexample plan.
